About Me

Hi, I'm Akil Maharjan

I'm a Frontend Developer and Frontend Instructor passionate about building modern, responsive, and user-friendly web applications.

I'm currently focused on building Orbitchat, a SaaS chatbot and customer communication platform, while continuously improving my skills in full-stack development, APIs, automation, and third-party integrations.

I also enjoy teaching frontend development and helping beginners understand web technologies through practical examples and projects.
